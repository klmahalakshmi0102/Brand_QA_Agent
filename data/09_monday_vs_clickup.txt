Monday.com vs ClickUp 2025: Which Platform Offers More for Your Money?

Monday.com and ClickUp are two of the fastest-growing project management platforms, and they compete directly for the same audience: teams that need more than Trello but find Jira too complex. We compare them across six key dimensions.

Ease of Use

Monday.com wins on first impressions. Its interface is clean, colorful, and immediately intuitive. Most teams can set up their first board and start working within an hour. The template gallery is well-organized, and the onboarding experience guides new users effectively.

ClickUp has a steeper learning curve. The sheer number of features and customization options can overwhelm new users. ClickUp acknowledges this and has invested in better onboarding, but reviewers still rate Monday.com higher for ease of setup.

Features

ClickUp wins on raw feature count. It includes built-in time tracking, documents, whiteboards, mind maps, goals, and chat — all features that Monday.com either lacks or offers through integrations. ClickUp's free tier alone includes more features than Monday.com's paid Basic plan.

Monday.com counters with better execution on core features. Its automations are easier to build, its dashboards are more visually polished, and its integrations feel more seamless. The quality vs quantity debate is real here.

Automations

Both platforms offer no-code automation builders. Monday.com's approach uses pre-built recipe templates that are easy to understand: "When status changes to X, do Y." ClickUp's automations are more flexible but slightly more complex to configure.

Monday.com offers 250 automation actions per month on the Standard plan and 25,000 on Pro. ClickUp offers 100 automations per month on Unlimited and 1,000 on Business. For automation-heavy teams, Monday.com provides more generous limits.

Pricing Comparison

Monday.com: Basic at $9/seat/month (3-seat minimum), Standard at $12, Pro at $19, Enterprise custom.
ClickUp: Free with unlimited tasks, Unlimited at $7/member/month, Business at $12, Enterprise custom.

ClickUp is consistently cheaper. The free tier alone is remarkably capable. A team of 10 on ClickUp Unlimited ($7/member) pays $70/month, while the same team on Monday.com Standard ($12/seat) pays $120/month. Over a year, that is $600 saved.

Reporting and Dashboards

Monday.com's dashboards are visually stunning and easy to configure. Drag-and-drop widgets include charts, numbers, timelines, and battery gauges. The dashboard experience is one of Monday.com's strongest selling points.

ClickUp's dashboards are functional but less polished. They offer similar widget types but require more configuration to look professional. For teams that present project data to stakeholders, Monday.com's dashboards make a better impression.

Integrations

Monday.com integrates with 200+ tools and the integration setup is straightforward. ClickUp also offers extensive integrations but its marketplace is less mature. Both integrate with essential tools like Slack, Google Workspace, GitHub, and Microsoft Teams.

Verdict

Choose Monday.com if your team values ease of use, visual polish, and powerful automations with minimal setup time. Choose ClickUp if your team wants maximum features at the lowest cost and is willing to invest time in learning the platform.

For budget-conscious startups: ClickUp. For mid-sized teams that want to get started fast: Monday.com.
